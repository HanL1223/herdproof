HerdProof demo v0.1. Synthetic data generated by a seeded generator, with fixtures shaped like the AgriWebb GraphQL, ISC NLIS and satellite pasture APIs. Footprints use a simplified port of the GAF beef method and are not a certified calculation. Names of farms, lenders and processors are fictional.
Emission equations and constants adapted from the AIA Environmental Accounting Platform emissions-calculators (Agricultural Innovation Australia, CC BY 4.0). Unofficial derivative, not validated by AIA.
